FAQ

Questions brokers ask us.

What is BrokerCore?

BrokerCore is a writing board built only for asset finance. You take a deal from the first enquiry through the fact-find, lender comparison and compliance to a settled commission claim, all in the one place.

Is it only for asset finance?

Yes. It is not a mortgage CRM stretched to fit. Every field, stage and workflow is shaped around how asset finance deals actually run, so there is nothing to work around.

Does it handle compliance?

The board tracks the NCCP steps for you. Privacy consent is required before a credit check, and a signed preliminary assessment before a consumer submission, so a deal cannot skip a step.

Which tools does it connect to?

Equifax for credit checks, illion for bank statements, the Australian Business Register for ABN lookups, and BrokerCore eSign for client signatures, all from inside the deal.

How does pricing work?

You choose a monthly plan, then pay only for add-ons like credit checks and bank statements as you use them. All prices exclude GST.

Can my whole brokerage use it?

Yes. Teams get admin controls, access levels and the option to run documents under your own brand. Get in touch and we will walk you through onboarding.
