How it works

Five steps from enquiry to commission.

One workflow, always moving forward. Nothing gets re-keyed, and nothing falls through.

Write the deal

Start from an enquiry. Client, asset and structure prefill, so a deal opens in seconds.

Build the fact-find

Income, expenses, assets, liabilities and objectives, with consolidated figures per client.

Compare lenders

Shortlist from live Lender Research and put at least three products side by side.

Stay compliant

Privacy, preliminary assessment and KYC, gated exactly where the NCCP requires it.

Settle & claim

Track to settlement, then claim your commission in a click. No spreadsheets.

The platform

Everything a deal needs, in one board.

Purpose-built for asset finance, not a mortgage CRM bent to fit.

Visual deal board

Drag a deal between stages, movement is always free. See your whole pipeline and what needs attention at a glance.

One fact-find, consolidated

Capture income, expenses, assets and liabilities once. Totals, net position and surplus roll up per client automatically.

Lender comparison

Pull rates and appetite straight from Lender Research and compare products side by side before you present.

Compliance, built in

Privacy consent before Equifax, a signed preliminary assessment before a consumer submission. The rules gate the actions for you.

Commission claims

Settled deals flow straight to a commission claim, prefilled and reconciled. Chase less, get paid sooner.

BrokerCore AI

A deal-aware assistant that drafts client emails, summarises a file and flags what is missing before submission. Always yours to review.
